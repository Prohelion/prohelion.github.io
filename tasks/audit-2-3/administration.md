## Section-level observations

- There is no `Administration/index.md`, and `Users_and_Access/` has no index either. Only `System_Configuration/` and `System_Configuration/Security/` have one. A reader arriving at the ADMIN section has no landing page that explains the pillars.
- None of the 27 pages declares `type:`, `owner:` or `last_reviewed:`, so the page-type rule cannot yet be applied or checked by tooling.
- Procedures are written as prose on 14 pages, against STYLE_GUIDE section 5. Only five pages contain any numbered list. No page has a check that a procedure worked, apart from a short verification note in Manage_Users.md.
- Four duplication clusters need one owner each, with the other pages linking to it: licensing (Licensing.md and Installation/Applying_a_Licence.md); kiosk (Kiosk_Mode.md, Profiles.md and How_To_Guides/Set_Up_Profinity_as_Kiosk.md); profile creation (Profiles.md and How_To_Guides/Create_New_Profile.md); MFA self-service (MFA_Account_Management.md and Two_Factor_Authentication.md).
- Repeated warnings: the remote-access warning appears in Licensing.md (twice), Profinity_Web.md and Installation/Windows_Installation.md. The "changing roles signs people out" warning appears in Manage_Users.md and Roles_and_Permissions.md. The "saving restarts Profinity" callout appears on seven tab pages.
- Factual conflict to resolve before release: Manage_Users.md and Roles_and_Permissions.md say Desktop Mode has no accounts or roles, while Licensing.md says a Desktop host with a Server licence creates the default admin account.
- Headings: 21 pages have at least one Title Case heading. "Related Documentation" is the heading on 15 pages and is Title Case, so the sentence-case rule would need "Related documentation" on each.
- Reviewer notes that apply: the restart note after applying a licence is missing from Licensing.md; the hardware section is already linked from Installation/Zip_Installation.md, but its heading still misleads; the "Machine Fingerprint / REST API could go" note is ambiguous and needs the reviewer to confirm the intent. Notes on Quick Start, Environment Variables, Custom Components and InfluxDB or Prometheus logging concern other sections and were not actioned here.
