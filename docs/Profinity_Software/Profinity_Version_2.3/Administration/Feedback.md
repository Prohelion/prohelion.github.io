# Feedback

Profinity includes a built-in feedback form for suggestions, issue reports and general comments about the product, which Prohelion uses to improve Profinity and to support its users.

## Accessing the Feedback Form

The feedback form is accessible through the **ADMIN** page in the Profinity interface. To access the form, open Profinity, select **ADMIN** in the side menu, and select the **Feedback** pill to open the feedback interface.

## Providing Effective Feedback

Each submission needs your full name, your contact email address and a detailed description of your feedback or issue. For a problem report, adding the steps to reproduce it, your operating system and Profinity version, and any error messages or screenshots helps Prohelion understand and resolve it faster.

## After You Submit

Once you submit your feedback, the Prohelion team reviews the submission and contacts you at the email address you supplied when more information is required.

## Alternative Support Options

For immediate assistance or urgent issues, visit the [Prohelion Support Portal](https://prohelion.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals) for support resources, or [contact Prohelion](https://www.prohelion.com/contact-us/) directly through the Prohelion website.
